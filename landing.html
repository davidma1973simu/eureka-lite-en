🔍 Reveal (R1-R5, 5 screens)

Dig out users' real pain, align with business goals — no wishful thinking

R1Describe your scenarioDescribe a concrete user scenario or challenge▸

ValueTurns a vague idea into an analyzable starting point, avoiding baseless assumptions
R2Explore the user journeyWalk the full flow from the user's perspective▸
You enterThe user's key actions before → during → after using
AI helpsPoints out key touchpoints, flags experience breakpoints, and fills in intermediate steps you hadn't thought of
You getA complete "user journey map" + key touchpoint findings
ValuePinpoints where users hurt the most, anchoring where to dig for needs later
R3Dig into user pain · FINDUse the four FIND steps to uncover deep needs — advance one primary POV at a time▸
You enterFill in screen by screen: Fact → Interpret → Need → Distill; open multiple findings, advancing one "primary POV" at a time while the rest are stashed
AI helpsGives examples and follow-up questions at every step, helping you dig from observation to deep need; mark each finding's POV as "primary" or "stashed"
You getA FIND-derived POV insight (one sentence that nails the user's real need) + a full list of all POVs
ValueSeparates "wishful needs" from "real needs"; one focus line at a time
R4Align business goalsMap stakeholder needs, generate testable business hypotheses▸
You enterReview the AI-identified stakeholders and score each need as feedback
AI helpsCalls a large language model to identify 4 stakeholder types with needs tightly related to your topic (12-point scale), and generates testable business hypotheses from your insight

ValueSee early who supports, who opposes, and whether the hypothesis holds up
R5Project briefSummarize your innovation assets (including the full POV list)▸
You enterReview / fine-tune each module's summary (insight, scenario and hypothesis are editable)

ValueCloses out the stage and distills reusable innovation assets
